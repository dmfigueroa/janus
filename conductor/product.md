# Product Guide: Janus

## Initial Concept

Janus is a premium, PWA-first media tracking application designed for users who want to organize their consumption of movies, TV shows, and more in chronological or release order. It bridges the gap between disparate media sources, offering a unified, high-aesthetic experience for tracking cinematic universes and cross-media franchises.

## Target Audience

- **Media Completionists**: Users dedicated to watching or consuming every entry in a franchise (e.g., Marvel, DC, Star Wars) in the correct order.
- **Casual Viewers**: Users who simply want a beautiful, easy-to-use tool to keep track of their "Up Next" list across different genres.
- **Aesthetic Enthusiasts**: Users who value high-end UI/UX, micro-animations, and a polished digital library feel.

## Core Value Proposition

- **Universal Media Hub**: A single source of truth for all media types—movies, TV shows, and planned expansions into games and books.
- **Chronological Mastery**: The primary tool for viewing and tracking franchises in their intended release or chronological order.
- **Premium Experience**: A local-first, high-performance PWA that feels like a native app on any device.

## Key Features

- **Timeline-Driven Navigation**: Track progress through a timeline of released content.
- **Multi-Watchlist Management**: Create and manage multiple watchlists for different goals or franchises.
- **API-First Data (TMDB, etc.)**: Robust search and metadata fetching for movies, TV, and soon games/books.
- **Local Persistence**: SQLite-powered storage for fast, reliable, and private data management.

## Strategic Roadmap (Upcoming Priorities)

- **Metadata Management**: Ability to manually fix or expand information that APIs provide incorrectly or incomplete.
- **List Interconnectivity**: Show shared items between different watchlists for better cross-reference.
- **Enhanced Management**: Full editing capabilities for entire series or individual items within a watchlist.
- **Search Optimization**: Refined search functionality within the watchlist to quickly find and filter tracked content.

## Platform Strategy

- **PWA-First**: Optimized for mobile browsers with full support for home-screen installation, ensuring accessibility and a native feel without the app store overhead.
