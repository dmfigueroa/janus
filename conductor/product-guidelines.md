# Product Guidelines: Janus

## Brand Voice & Tone

- **Professional & Direct**: Communication should be clear and efficient, respecting the user's time and focus.
- **Supportive & Guiding**: Help the user discover new media and organize their franchises without being intrusive.
- **Premium & Tactile**: The language should reflect the high-end, realistic, and tactile nature of the application.

## Design Language (Skeuomorphic PWA)

- **Realistic Aesthetic**: Focus on skeuomorphic design principles—textures, depth, and lighting that create an almost tangible feeling (e.g., leather, wood, metal, paper).
- **Tactile Interactions**: Use animations and transitions that mimic physical interactions (e.g., clicking a button that depresses, flipping a page, sliding a drawer).
- **Typography**: Classic, high-quality fonts that feel part of the environment (e.g., etched into metal, printed on paper).
- **Single-Focused Aesthetic**: Prioritize the realistic look over multi-theme support. The application will have a fixed, high-quality appearance that emphasizes realism.

## UX Principles

- **Mobile-First/PWA**: Every interaction must be optimized for touch, with proper safe-area handling and large enough tap targets.
- **Local-First Performance**: Interactions should be instantaneous. Use optimistic updates and smooth loading states for API-driven content.
- **Realistic Feedback**: Provide visual and haptic feedback that reinforces the tactile nature of the design.
- **Physical Hierarchy**: Use depth and layering to represent the hierarchy of information, as if the elements were physically placed on top of each other.

## Accessibility

- **High Legibility**: Ensure text is clearly readable against the realistic textures.
- **Screen Reader Support**: Use semantic HTML and ARIA labels for complex UI components.
- **Consistent Lighting**: Use a single, consistent light source for shadows and highlights to maintain the illusion of depth.
